### **Error: "ALLOWED_HOSTS validation error"**

**Cause:** Request host not in ALLOWED_HOSTS

**Fix in settings_prod.py:**
```python
RENDER_SERVICE = os.getenv("RENDER_EXTERNAL_URL", "")

    RENDER_SERVICE = RENDER_SERVICE.replace("https://", "").replace("http://", "")

    ROOT_DOMAIN,
    f".{ROOT_DOMAIN}",
```
