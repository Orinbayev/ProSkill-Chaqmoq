Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish so'rovi / Data Deletion Request

O'zbekcha

ChaqmoqApp ilovasi va platformasidan shaxsiy ma'lumotlaringizni o'chirtirish uchun quyidagi qadamlarni bajaring:

  1. Quyidagi manzilga elektron xat yuboring: pragrammeruzz@gmail.com
  2. Xat mavzusiga "Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish" deb yozing
  3. Xatda ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tgan email manzilingiz yoki telefon raqamingizni ko'rsating

Yoki o'zingiz tahsil olayotgan / farzandingiz o'qiyotgan o'quv markaziga bevosita murojaat qilishingiz mumkin — markaz administratori hisobingizni o'chirib beradi.

Qanday ma'lumotlar o'chiriladi

  • Ism-familiya, email manzil, telefon raqam(lar)
  • Hisob (akkaunt) va unga bog'liq profil ma'lumotlari
  • Davomat va o'quv faoliyati tarixi

Qanday ma'lumotlar saqlanib qolishi mumkin

  • Moliyaviy hujjatlar (to'lov yozuvlari) — qonunchilik talablari bo'yicha buxgalteriya hisobi uchun 3 yilgacha saqlanishi mumkin, shundan so'ng o'chiriladi

So'rovingiz 30 kun ichida ko'rib chiqiladi va bajariladi.

English

To request deletion of your personal data from the ChaqmoqApp app and platform, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to: pragrammeruzz@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  3. Include the email address or phone number registered in the app

Alternatively, you may contact your education center directly — the center administrator can delete your account.

Data that will be deleted

  • Full name, email address, phone number(s)
  • Account and related profile data
  • Attendance and learning activity history

Data that may be retained

  • Financial records (payment history) may be retained for up to 3 years for accounting and legal compliance, after which they are deleted

Requests are processed within 30 days.
